Meet Chloe

I started CLC in 2012 after a BA (Hons) in Health Promotion & Fitness, then trained in laser, skin care and aesthetics so I could look after the whole picture: how you look and how you feel.

One-off treatments rarely last, so every plan I make is built around you, for results that hold. I'll always explain each step, answer every question, and take things at your pace, especially if you're nervous of needles.

I use the most advanced non-surgical products and tools available, keep up with regulation as it changes, and keep my prices fair. Looking great is really about feeling great, and everyone deserves to feel confident in their skin.
